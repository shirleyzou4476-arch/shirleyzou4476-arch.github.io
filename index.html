About

At the keyboard since the age of four, now performing concerti with regional orchestras and debuting on the Carnegie Hall stage.

Shirley is a student in the Piano Department at the High School for the Performing and Visual Arts (HSPVA) in Houston. An active soloist and concerto performer, she has earned top honors across regional, national, and international competitions, and continues to build a career spanning solo, collaborative, and concerto performance.

Current teacher Dr. Matthew Loudermilk
Previously studied with Alina Uddin, and Timothy Hester — Edythe Bates Old Professor of Piano, University of Houston Moores School of Music
